          How to read this, and terms used
          • Score is Siteimprove’s Title II target: how many Level A, Level AA and ARIA checks a site passes. 100% meets the goal. It shows progress, not legal compliance.
          • Overall score is Siteimprove’s DCI score. It also counts optional checks, so it is usually lower.
          • Issues (occurrences) counts every time a required check fails on a page.
          • Ready for manual testing: a WordPress site with a confirmed score of 98% or higher and a working last check.
          • Level A and Level AA are the WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) levels Title II requires. ARIA is code that tells screen readers what custom controls do. Best practices are optional suggestions.
          • Missing data is shown as “—” and is never counted as zero. Test and development sites are always hidden.

          Siteimprove data is pulled every night at 2 a.m. Detroit time. The pull time is not the time Siteimprove last checked a site.
