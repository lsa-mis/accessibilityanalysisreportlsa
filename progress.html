    How these numbers work, and terms used
    • Matched sites: a confirmed score on both dates. All changes use only these.
    • Score: Siteimprove’s Title II target (Level A, AA and ARIA). Ready for manual testing at 93%+ (LSA AEM) or 98%+ (others). Not proof of compliance.
    • Issues (occurrences): each time a required check fails on a page. A drop is a net change, not verified fixes.
    • Points: percentage points (96.2% to 96.5% is +0.3).
    • Needs reliable data: estimated scores, failed checks or older imports. Never counted as zero.
    • Reliable data starts with snapshots from October 26, 2026, which record scan quality details.
